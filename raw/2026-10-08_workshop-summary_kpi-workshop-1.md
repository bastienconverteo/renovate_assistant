Hi All, 

Thank you for joining the call. Please find the summary of this conversation: 
1. ‎US KPI catalog will be the baseline for Canada. The Canada team will review the existing US KPI definitions and identify:
KPIs that can be adopted as-is
KPIs requiring Canada-specific adjustments
Missing/new KPIs required for Canada. 
 
2. The initial focus should be on the consumer funnel, KPI name and KPI definition. Additional dimensions such as MTD, YTD, YoY and monthly views can be addressed subsequently. 
 
3. Canada has already started a preliminary comparison. Bastien has reviewed several KPIs against Canada’s reporting requirements and will expand the assessment once feedback is received from other teams, including SFMC and Genie.
 
4. A dedicated D2C KPI Catalog for Canada will be created. Canada-specific definitions or additional requirements should be captured there. One example discussed was Canada’s requirement for a 3-month period, in addition to periods such as MTD/YTD. 
Gold will become the primary consumption layer. As legacy CRM Base/CRM Reporting access goes away, downstream consumers such as Genie, SFMC and CDP will need to consume the required data from Gold. Therefore, required KPIs and attributes need to be adequately represented in the Gold layer. 
The team also needs to review Gold-layer attributes, not only KPIs. The US Gold-layer structure can serve as a starting point, but Canada needs to determine the attributes it requires. Fatima noted that the US exercise started with attributes first and KPIs later, although sequencing for Canada should depend on priorities. 
5. Global Standardization vs. Canada-Specific Requirements

6. The overall direction is to maintain as much consistency as possible across US, Canada and LATAM, supporting the broader goal of a single consumer profile/source. Existing US definitions should therefore be adopted where they work rather than creating multiple market-specific versions.
